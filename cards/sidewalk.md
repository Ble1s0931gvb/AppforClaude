# sidewalk

- **Transcription:** /ˈsaɪd.wɔːk/ (US) · /ˈsaɪd.wɔːk/ (UK)
- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Translation (RU):** тротуар
- **Definition:** a paved path for pedestrians beside a road.
- **UK/US:** US *sidewalk* = UK *pavement*
- **Word parts:** side + walk
- **Examples:**
  - Don't ride your bike on the sidewalk.
  - She waited on the sidewalk outside the café.
- **Collocations:** sidewalk café · sidewalk sale · sidewalk chalk · hit the sidewalk
- **Synonyms:** pavement (UK), footpath
- **Association:** side of the road + walk = where you walk by the road
